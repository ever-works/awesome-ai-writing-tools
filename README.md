## Editing & Grammar

- [Grammarly](https://www.grammarly.com/) - Grammarly is an AI-powered writing assistant that checks grammar, spelling, punctuation, and style. It offers browser extensions, desktop apps, and integrations with popular platforms. A free plan is available, with premium and business tiers for advanced features. ([Read more](/details/grammarly.md)) `AI-Powered` `Freemium` `Free Plan` `API` `Enterprise`
- [Hemingway Editor](https://hemingwayapp.com/) - Hemingway Editor highlights complex sentences, passive voice, and readability issues in text. It is available as a free web app and a paid desktop application. The tool focuses on improving prose clarity rather than generating content. ([Read more](/details/hemingway-editor.md)) `Freemium` `Free Plan` `Readability`
- [LanguageTool](https://languagetool.org/) - LanguageTool is a multilingual grammar, style, and spell checker with open-source core. It supports self-hosting and offers API access for integrations. A free web version exists, with premium desktop and enterprise options. ([Read more](/details/languagetool.md)) `Open Source` `Self-Hosted` `Freemium` `Free Plan` `API` `Multilingual`
- [ProWritingAid](https://prowritingaid.com/) - ProWritingAid is a grammar and style checker for writers and editors. It provides in-depth reports on readability, overused words, and sentence structure. A free version is available, with premium and business licenses. ([Read more](/details/prowritingaid.md)) `AI-Powered` `Freemium` `Free Plan` `API`

## Writing Assistants

- [HyperWrite](https://www.hyperwrite.ai/) - HyperWrite is an AI writing assistant that provides autocomplete, rewriting, and content generation. It operates as a browser extension and web editor. A free tier is offered, with premium subscriptions for unlimited use. ([Read more](/details/hyperwrite.md)) `AI-Powered` `Freemium` `Free Plan` `Browser Extension`
- [Lex](https://lex.page/) - Lex is a modern word processor with built-in AI writing assistance. It offers autocomplete, feedback, and collaborative editing for documents. A free plan is available, with paid options for advanced AI features. ([Read more](/details/lex.md)) `AI-Powered` `Freemium` `Free Plan` `Collaboration`
- [Notion AI](https://www.notion.com/) - Notion AI is an integrated AI assistant within Notion for drafting, summarizing, and editing pages. It helps generate content directly inside workspaces and databases. Access requires a Notion subscription with an AI add-on. - [Rytr](https://rytr.me/) - Rytr is an AI writing assistant for emails, blogs, and social media content. It supports multiple languages and tones with a simple editor. A free tier is available, with paid plans for unlimited usage. ([Read more](/details/rytr.md)) `AI-Powered` `Freemium` `Free Plan`
